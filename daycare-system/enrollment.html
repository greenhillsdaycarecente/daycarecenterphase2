Greenhills Phase II Daycare Center — Enrollment Form

Terms and Conditions & Parent/Guardian Consent

Please read the information below before accessing the enrollment form.

1. About the Greenhills Phase II Daycare Center Enrollment System

The Greenhills Phase II Daycare Center Enrollment System is a web-based system used to collect, organize, manage, and process information needed for daycare enrollment. It helps the Daycare Center manage enrollment applications, child and guardian records, documents, class/session assignment, application status, communication, and related administrative activities.

2. Information Collected

The enrollment form may collect information about the child and parent/guardian, including:

  • Child's name, date of birth, gender, address, and nickname;
  • Child photo and submitted document information, when provided;
  • Parent/guardian name, relationship, contact number, and address; and
  • Emergency contact information needed for daycare records and communication.

3. Purpose of Using the Information

The information will be used only for legitimate daycare-related purposes, such as processing enrollment applications, maintaining child records, assigning classes or sessions, communicating with parents/guardians, managing daycare services, preparing reports, and carrying out necessary administrative functions of the Daycare Center.

4. Access, Storage, and Protection of Information

Information submitted through the system will be stored in the system's database and may be accessed by authorized Daycare Center personnel who need the information to perform their assigned responsibilities. Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to help protect the information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

5. Parent/Guardian Responsibility

The parent/guardian is responsible for providing accurate and truthful information and for ensuring that they are authorized to provide the child's information for enrollment. If information changes or an error is discovered, the parent/guardian should inform the Daycare Center so the record can be corrected when appropriate.

6. Parent/Guardian Consent

By selecting “I Agree and Continue”, you confirm that you are the parent/guardian or an authorized representative of the child identified in the enrollment application. You voluntarily consent to the Greenhills Phase II Daycare Center Enrollment System collecting, storing, accessing, and using the child's and guardian's information provided in the enrollment form for the daycare-related purposes described above.

Your consent is required before you can access and submit the enrollment form through this system. If you select “I Do Not Agree”, you will not be allowed to proceed with enrollment through the system.

Please check the box to enable “I Agree and Continue”.
